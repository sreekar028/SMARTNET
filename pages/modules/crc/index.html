CRC Simulation

Cyclic Redundancy Check · Error Detection

Cyclic Redundancy Check (CRC) is an error-detection technique widely used in digital networks and storage. The sender divides the data by a generator polynomial using modulo-2 binary division. The remainder is appended to the data as the CRC value. The receiver performs the same division on the received codeword — a zero remainder means no error was detected.

Algorithm Steps

1 Append (n−1) zeros to the data, where n is the generator length.
2 Perform modulo-2 binary division of the appended data by the generator.
3 The remainder is the CRC value.
4 The transmitted codeword = original data + CRC remainder.
